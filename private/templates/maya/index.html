About

I started in graphic design and drifted into the browser when I realised type, grids and motion could respond to people. That sensibility still drives how I build: interfaces should feel inevitable, not impressive.

Day to day I work across the stack of the front end — component architecture, rendering performance, motion design and accessibility — usually as the person translating between design and engineering.
